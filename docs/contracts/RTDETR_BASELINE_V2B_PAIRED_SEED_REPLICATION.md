# Baseline-v2b paired seed resolution replication

The owner conditionally authorizes seeds 1 and 2, declared before their results,
with physical batch 8, accumulation 2 and exactly 30 epochs at each of 640 and
896. Seed 0 remains the existing matched control. Only train_core and development
are allowed. No held-out access, data-protocol change, longer training, other
resolution or trainable sparse method is authorized by this phase.

## Evidence before execution

The authenticated seed-0 development analysis must pass the frozen qualification:
positive corrected primary diagonal AP difference, positive COCO AP-small
difference with a positive paired-cluster 95% lower endpoint, fewer unmatched
small GT at IoU 0.5, and complete engineering and native hardware evidence.
A successful audit alone does not imply eligibility; the execution gate also
requires eligible_for_fixed_paired_seed1_and_seed2 to be true.

The qualification binds its analysis leaves, primary evaluator certification,
original conditional authorization and the consuming committed source identity.
Complete verification is performed before campaign preparation. Worker admission
also rechecks the small identity and threshold witnesses and complete-file leaf
hashes without repeating the bootstrap or loading large caches before monitoring.

A clean archive must verify the exact candidate tree with the unchanged applicable
skip inventory. Source, configuration, pretrained bytes, data authorities, all
30 epoch sampler digests, capacity plans, hardware policy and unique invocation
identities are frozen before any formal cell. The existing training core,
optimizer, loss normalization, deterministic sampling and restore tolerances
are unchanged.

## Fixed sequence and clocks

All four capacities finish first. Each cell then runs four smoke logical windows
and a new-process restore from update 2, replaying windows 3 and 4. Every cell's
restored model, optimizer, RNG, loss, BN/DN and update clocks must pass the existing
comparison. Within each seed, the 896 smoke matches the 640 initialization and
logical sample receipts.

Only after every capacity and smoke/replay closure passes does the controller
record formal-start evidence and execute, sequentially:
seed1/640, seed1/896, seed2/640, seed2/896. Each 896 formal cell matches the complete
same-seed 640 logical sequence and initial learned weights. Seeds are not selected
by interim results. Capacity and smoke states are disposable; each formal worker
initializes anew from the frozen seed and pretrained authority.

The actual training epoch indices are 1 through 30. Each epoch has 304 logical
updates from 4,869 source images with five dropped. The fixed endpoint has 9,120
optimizer, EMA and warmup updates and 18,240 physical microsteps. Development
metrics at epochs 10 and 20 are health observations; epoch 30 is the primary
comparison endpoint. Every completed epoch has its own checkpoint.

An Adam parameter state advances only when its gradient participates. In
particular, the denoising embedding can have no gradient when both physical
microbatches contain no targets. Capacity auditing records named participation
and verifies the step increment against it; it does not demand every parameter
state exist and advance on every window.

## Capacity, hardware and failure evidence

Each seed's frozen plan includes the early windows, predeclared high-density
windows, and separately labelled synthetic target-density envelopes. It retains
full Python integer augmentation seeds and verifies the complete 30 epoch order.
Development capacity includes the full fixed evaluation stream. Both native
sampled free memory and conservative reserved-peak headroom must remain at least
3,072 MiB.

Every worker receives fresh exclusive GPU admission, with the strict Xorg identity
allowlist, unchanged boot/GPU/driver identity, startup BERT baseline and incremental
hardware error checks. EDAC is explicitly unavailable where it cannot be observed.
The administrator's external 1500/1500 MHz setting is bound through the receipt,
hardware identity and loaded runtime samples. The worker never sets or resets
clocks. This is sampled compliance evidence, not an unavailable getter's proof of
a permanent hard cap.

Any foreign compute, frequency above 1500 MHz, observation gap, new hardware
error, reset/reboot, OOM, nonfinite value or sample/restore inconsistency stops
the campaign. Only the controller's identified owned process may be signalled.
There is no automatic retry or configuration fallback. Source changes after the
formal-start marker require stopping; results from different frozen
implementations must not be spliced into a pair. All failed sites are preserved.

## Fixed-epoch reporting

Uniform corrected primary AP is recomputed from saved epoch-30 predictions with
the certified evaluator. Historical formal-GT primary and COCO AP, AP-small and
AR-small remain separate columns. The complete lineage-backed ignore semantics
and their original raw-byte provenance limit remain explicit.

Report seed 0, 1 and 2 separately, each 896-minus-640 difference, mean difference,
sample standard deviation and range. Pairing checks cover initialization and all
logical batches, and costs include wall time, memory, checkpoints and native
hardware evidence. No best epoch or best seed substitutes for the fixed endpoint.
Development bootstrap intervals condition on the fitted checkpoints and do not
measure training-seed uncertainty. Three seed pairs support an exploratory
estimate of seed variation, not held-out certification.

The final recommendation may compare extending to 60 epochs with a sparse
prototype. Neither action starts automatically from this campaign.
